New · geometry

Pleasure is not the negative of pain

A bipolar valence code would make the two directions point in opposite ways. They don't: their cosine is positive in every model (Mistral-7B v0.1 +0.24, Mistral-7B-Instruct v0.1 +0.23, Qwen2.5-7B +0.30, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct +0.31, Qwen3-8B +0.25). The overlap sits on the matched-domain diagonal of a category-by-category comparison; physical pleasure and physical pain are the only pair that point slightly apart.

Splitting the two unit vectors into their sum (a shared axis) and their difference (a sign axis) puts every stimulus set on a plane. Topic-matched neutral sentences (neutral social exchanges, rules, problem steps, being assessed) and neutral high-arousal events stay near zero on the shared axis, so it does not encode topic or arousal; it encodes how much an event matters to the speaker, whichever way it goes. The sign axis carries valence.
